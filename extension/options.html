TypeSafe API key

Jev, TypeSafe's classification model, decides whether a post is a take on AI and whether its author is paid by AI. Get a key from console.typesafe.ai.

How sure Jev has to be

Jev has to be this sure of both things: that the post is a take on AI, and that its author is paid by AI, going by their profile and what they say in the post. Higher means fewer notes and fewer mistakes. Well-known accounts that say nothing about themselves need 90%, whatever is set here.

What leaves your browser

A post goes to TypeSafe's API at api.typesafe.ai, with your key, when it or its author's profile shows signs of being about AI. What the author's public profile shows goes with it: display name, handle, bio, profile link, affiliation badge and professional category. Once an author is found to be paid by AI, their later posts are sent too, whatever they are about, for the next 30 days. Other posts are never sent. Nothing about your own X account is sent, and the extension makes no requests to X of its own: it reads the profiles X already loads with each post. Your key is stored in this browser only and is never synced.
